## Appended 2026-08-25 — the owner's 2D-BENCHMARK correction path, mapped onto what T6 already ran

> **Owner, 2026-08-25:** *"we compute the coordinates ratios of the projections of
> the palm landmarks in 2D for a series of palm orientations, based on typical palm
> dimension ratios: those coordinates ratios become benchmarks; the x, y, z
> synthetic coordinates are projected in 2D and are matched to the series of
> benchmarks; best match provides the best estimation of palm orientation and
> determines the correction to be applied to eliminate the bias."* And: *"I do not
> know if this path was already investigated in T6. We can revisit it after F1."*

⭐ **ANSWER: yes, in substance — §2.0.11 and §2.0.12 of the investigation log.**
Recorded here so the revisit starts from the measurements rather than repeating
them.

### The mapping, step by step

| the proposal | what was already run |
|---|---|
| benchmarks of 2D projected palm ratios over a series of orientations | §2.0.12 did the **continuous** form: the **2×2 shape map that carries the canonical palm onto the observed pixels**. Its SVD gives tilt **magnitude** (`σ₂/σ₁ = cos θ_true`) and **direction** (`φ_true`) directly, **depth-free**. A benchmark table is the *discrete* version of that same computation — it adds quantisation and gains nothing |
| ratios rather than absolute sizes, so hand size cancels | ⭐ Same reasoning, same fix — §2.0.12 chose the **ratio** over projected area precisely because *"area conflates compression with distance"* |
| typical palm dimension ratios as the model | measured: forcing a session's **own** shape instead of the canonical one moved yaw only **29.3° → 27.5°** (T6 §9 step 6). Per-user shape is **not** the missing variable |
| best match → true orientation → derive the correction | §2.0.12 → §2.0.16 → **T6d**, which is exactly *"keep Horn, correct the tilt"*. Built, live-tested over four sessions, **owner-rejected** as a *"very minor improvement"* |

### ⛔ What the measurements closed

* **21 761 frames / 58 sessions** (§2.0.11) then **19 064 / 62** (§2.0.12), which
  is corpus scale, not a scripted handful.
* ⭐ **The good news it produced**: MediaPipe's tilt **direction is right**
  (median 10.6° vs 45° chance) — the defect is **one corrupt scalar per frame**,
  not a broken 3D orientation.
* ⛔ **The bad news**: the map from (true tilt, direction) to measured tilt **is
  not a law**. Best model **RMS 9.6°, ~51% of variance** — *"far too coarse to
  invert into a rotation fix"*. Per-frame IQR is **16–41°** at every true tilt.
* ⛔ **Per-session calibration cannot rescue it**: 9.6° → 8.5°, session constants
  explain **12%**. **The residual is WITHIN-session**, so no calibration gesture,
  however good, reaches it.
* ⚠ One subtlety in the proposal as literally written: projecting **MediaPipe's
  own x,y,z** to 2D and matching that recovers only the orientation its 3D
  already implies — which we have directly. **The information is in the OBSERVED
  pixels**, which is what §2.0.12 used. (Comparing the two *is* meaningful, but
  T6 measured reprojection RMS at **2.1–2.6 px** — the model fits; the z is what
  is wrong.)

### ⭐⭐ WHAT IS GENUINELY STILL OPEN — and why revisiting AFTER `F1` is the right call

§2.0.12 closed the regression line **for the features it tried**, and named four
it did not: **hand distance, in-plane rotation, FINGER CONFIGURATION, detector
confidence** (*"none is obviously promising"*).

⭐⭐ **`F1` makes one of them measurable for the first time.** A fingertip-driven
transform produces exactly the finger-configuration state that was unavailable
when §2.0.12 ran. So *"revisit after `F1`"* is not merely a scheduling
preference — **`F1` supplies a candidate feature for the half of the distortion
that is still unexplained.** ⚠ Half of it *is* explained by tilt and direction
already; the question is whether finger configuration holds any of the rest.

⚠ **And the acceptance bar is not "better RMS"** — T6d had the best numbers of
any correction and was still rejected, because the two A/B panels differed by a
median **4.83°**, flat across every palm-tilt band, **below what an eye resolves
on a 40–80 px cube**. Any revisit must clear *visible* improvement, not just
measured.
