## Appended 2026-08-25 — ⛔⛔ THE MODEL CARD SAYS FINGERTIPS ARE THE WORST LANDMARKS

**Read this before designing `F1`. It was not known when the row was written.**
MediaPipe's own Model Card (archived under `60_SECURITY_COMPLIANCE/evidence/`)
reports, from Google's own fairness evaluation:

> *"We found that per-joint MNAE is the smallest at the base of each finger, and
> gets larger toward the fingertip. We conjecture that the prediction is easier
> around the palm which is more rigid than the fingers."* — and *"The findings
> are consistent across all regions."*

⛔⛔ **`F1` proposes driving the object's whole transform from exactly the
landmarks the model estimates WORST.** That does not kill the row — the owner's
reason for it stands, and the palm genuinely is too coarse for assembly-style
alignment — but it reframes the design problem:

* the palm was not chosen for convenience, it was chosen because **it is rigid,
  and the model is most accurate there**;
* fingertip motion is the signal `F1` wants, and it arrives with **the highest
  per-joint error in the model**;
* ⚠ *"the normalized absolute error is larger for blurry or occluded joints"* —
  and a hand **closing around an object occludes its own fingertips**.

⭐ **So the design must treat tip positions as NOISY MEASUREMENTS, not as truth**
— filtering, contact-consistency, or a bounded trim on top of the palm's coarse
pose, rather than a fit that trusts them equally. ⚠ This is a second, independent
reason not to build a rigid-body fit over palm+tips (the first is that the arm is
A10-dead twice).

⛔⛔ **AND THE CARD PUTS THIS GAME'S CORE ACTION OUT OF SCOPE:**

> *"OUT-OF-SCOPE APPLICATIONS. Not appropriate for: … Predicting hand landmarks
> with gloves or occlusions. **For example when the hand is holding objects** or
> there is decoration on the hand including jewelry, tattoo and henna."*

⚠ **The entire game is a hand holding an object.** That is not a reason to stop —
it demonstrably works well enough to have shipped snap/translate/rotate — but it
is the honest frame for every landmark-quality defect in the queue (`T1`, `T2`,
`N12`), and it means **upstream landmark quality has a ceiling that no estimator
work can lift**. §16.17 already says a jump both estimators reproduce is in the
landmarks; the card says why.
